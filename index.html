Developer experience

From payload
to response.

Make a request, receive a rendered PNG—without a second fetch or persistent storage.

$ curl --request POST http://localhost:8080/api/v1/qr/url \
  --header 'X-API-Key: your-api-key' \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data '{"url":"https://example.com","show_logo":true}'
